# Music Box Project

Music player chosen out of around 10 submissions for the FISD Career and Technology Education Center 2025 Showcase. The theme of the showcase was "Food Trucks", so this music player allows the user to choose a theme and design based off of six different food trucks - Burgers & Sliders, Pizza, Dessert, Breakfast, Crepes, and Tacos & Burritos. The user can then choose songs from their library to create and edit playlists, choose the order of the songs on the playlist, and, most importantly, play their music. Additional functionality includes puasing and skipping songs and looping the playlist.
